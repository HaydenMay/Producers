# V0.7 Decisions, Outcomes and Saves Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Make the existing movie loop understandable and resumable on a phone without changing simulation rules.

**Architecture:** Keep the bottom sheet and core decisions. Derive estimates, actor fit, offers, release economics and cash activity from existing core state; store a versioned decision replay that restores exact RNG and validates each action against the current rules. Pause the clock on restore.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript, Vite, Three.js, Vitest, browser localStorage.

**Spec:** User-supplied Producers V0.7 work prompt (2026-11-14) and `docs/DESIGN_LEDGER.md`.

## Constraints and review focus

- Preserve all routes, scene, camera and single Menu button; no economy or scoring changes.

- Warn only for cost risk and keep the breakdown behind a tap; do not hide contracts.
- Restore pending decisions, RNG, release history and contract progress; never overwrite invalid saves without consent.
- Distinguish movie costs from actual cash activity, and reserve transfers from cash transactions.
- Keep keyboard, reduced motion and clock suspension functional; verify phone and desktop layouts.
