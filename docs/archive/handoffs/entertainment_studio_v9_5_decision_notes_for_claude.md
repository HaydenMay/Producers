# Entertainment Studio Tycoon — v9.5 Decision Notes for Claude

**Purpose:** Apply one newly confirmed Star-relationship rule, clean up stale review prompts, and preserve a balance-test concern. This is a targeted amendment, **not** a redesign or authorization to implement.

## 1. LOCKED — When Star relationships decrease

A Star's relationship with the studio **decreases only when the studio replaces that Star in an established role for a new season or sequel of the same IP**.

- **Penalty:** Riley leads *Dead Frequency* Season 1. The studio chooses Jordan to replace Riley as the lead in Season 2. Riley's relationship with the studio decreases.
- **Penalty:** Riley leads the original *Dead Frequency* movie. The studio makes a direct sequel, recasting Riley's role with Jordan. Riley's relationship decreases.
- **No penalty:** Riley starred in *Dead Frequency*, but the studio casts Jordan in an unrelated comedy. Riley was not replaced in an existing role.
- **No penalty:** Riley is not cast in the studio's next unrelated project, or the studio simply takes a break from working with Riley.
- **No direct relationship penalty for a flop or falling Fame.** Fame and career outcomes may change independently, but they do not automatically lower the Star–studio relationship. This supersedes v9.5's language that relationships drop when a project "hurts their career."

Existing positive relationship progression (e.g. developing a Star's career) and the relationship-based fee discount remain. Exact relationship changes and discount sizes are balancing questions, not locked numbers.

**Scope boundary:** The agreed recasting trigger covers **new seasons and direct sequels**. Do not silently expand it to reboots, unrelated projects, or merely hiring a different Star; reboot treatment can be decided later if necessary.

**Update locations in v9.5:** §6 “Star relationships,” the overview/summary of Star pricing and relationships, and the decision ledger. Ensure those passages agree.

## 2. EDITORIAL CLEANUP — Old review prompts are not new open decisions

In §19A–19B, remove, archive, or explicitly label superseded questions that have already been resolved by v9–v9.5. In particular, do **not** reopen rigid Small/Medium/Large budget tiers, the separate Star-fee accounting, the basic distributor-cut rules, self-funding reach, IP-level Fans, streaming build timing, or established TV unlock/announcement rules. Keep genuinely unresolved questions visible (for example, whether TV renewal terms carry over) without suggesting settled decisions are up for debate.

This is documentation hygiene, not a request for new mechanics.

## 3. RECOMMENDATION — Later balance test, not a new mechanic

When we simulate several projects, examine the combined quality growth from:

1. Staff improving their four universal production stats;
2. Stars improving their genre-family skills within their level-based point budgets;
3. The studio's genre experience increasing production quality.

The risk is that all three bonuses compound until excellent reviews become routine. **Do not delete or nerf any of these systems preemptively.** Test their combined effect once sample production math exists.

## Preserve existing locked decisions

Keep IP-level Fans, the three main progression economies, pre-display affordable Star audition fallback, distributor advances/royalties and retained surplus, coherent connected release outcomes, two starting employees, and optional once-per-save opening tutorial intact. No engine or implementation decision is being made in this amendment.
