# Portrait game preview

The early game preview is served at `/Producers/game/`. The visual scene lab remains at `/Producers/scene/`, the character lab at `/Producers/`, and the previous gameplay prototype at `/Producers/studio/`. Run `npm run dev:game-preview` locally.

The top bar shows the existing simulation's date, cash and prestige. The bottom Menu button opens a dismissible sheet with an upward animation (disabled for reduced-motion preferences). Projects and pending decisions are accessible through the menu. Projects lets the user pick an available new-movie genre and theme, play/pause or step production, choose a star and distributor when prompted, and see the release's audience and critic scores. Between productions, users can advance a week or take an available work-for-hire job; jobs advance one week at a time and use the existing simulation decisions. People shows the simulation's real staff and four stats; Studio shows cash, prestige, and current staff capacity. Menus and choices use the existing `PrototypeGame` and `ProductionClock`; no simulation rules are changed.

V0.7 adds production approaches and cost estimates, casting and distribution comparisons, release performance, and studio cash activity. All values and decisions come from the simulation; distributor advertising is not a studio expense. Releases remain accessible during other work from the Menu. The preview saves one career in the current browser on the current device; it is not cloud sync, and clearing browser data can remove it. Reloading restores the career paused. An incompatible or damaged save is retained until the player confirms New studio. Very long careers that exceed the replay save limit stop saving with a warning and preserve the previous save.

Hiring, training, procedural portraits in the menus, studio upgrades, expansion, and the remaining menus are not yet integrated in this screen. Casting uses stable initial avatars for now. The initial staff members get stable generated appearances keyed to their IDs; their animations are illustrative rather than a complete representation of staff activities. The scene is currently limited to six visible desk/person slots, so it does not yet represent the planned eight-person studio. Existing simulation values, including costs, remain testing values where indicated in the design ledger. The traditional distribution versus Originals campaign question remains open (PROD-111).
